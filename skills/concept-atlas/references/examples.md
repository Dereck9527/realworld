## Ambiguity example

Input: `What is “network”?` Ask whether this means computer networks, social networks, neural networks, or another field because the genealogy and prerequisite map materially differ. In contrast, for `entropy in thermodynamics`, state the assumption and proceed.

## Verified shape

A verified report should say its cutoff, put every consequential historical assertion in the ledger with inspected source IDs, use `scholarly dispute` where priority is contested, and distinguish an event date from a source publication date. It must not claim that a source list alone proves any statement.
